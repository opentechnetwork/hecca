05

Usage

Clear space = 1 × cap height
H
HECCA
Hybrid By Design

Minimum size

24px crest height on screen. 0.5in on print. Below that, use the monogram alone.

Clear space

One full letter-height of empty space on all four sides. Nothing crosses it — no text, no borders, no product edges.

Approved backgrounds

Deep Black, Soft White, Stripe Beige, Deep Plum, Emerald. Photography only where contrast is verified.

Glow

One halo only, never both. Glow is for screen, foil, and signage — not for embroidery or small print.

Gold is constant

The wordmark is never recoloured to pink, plum, emerald, or tan. Those are halo and field colours only.

Not permitted

No stretching, rotating, outlining, drop shadows, gradients across the letters, or added words inside the lockup.
